Our Story

Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23rd September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). Headquartered in Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates across India with a strong network of dedicated professionals, volunteers and corporate partners.

The foundation believes in the power of collective action — every contribution is directed towards essential causes such as food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and social inclusion programmes across India.

Through six flagship projects — SEVA, BACHPANSHALA, JEEV, UDAAN, PRAKRITI and VIKAS — InAmigos works to build a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society, while its transparent operations and strong social-media presence keep supporters informed and engaged.

Quick Facts & Credentials

  • Founded: 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla
  • Section 8 registered non-profit, licensed by Central Government
  • 80G & 12A certified — tax-exempt benefits for donors
  • CSR-1 registered — open to corporate CSR partnerships
  • NGO Darpan registered (NITI Aayog, Govt. of India)
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality standards
  • 50,000+ beneficiaries reached across India
What We Do

Our Ongoing Projects

Six flagship initiatives tackling India's most pressing social challenges — from hunger and education to women empowerment, animal welfare, environment and employability.

🍛

PROJECT SEVA

"Serving Humanity with Compassion"

Provides nutritious food and warm clothing to the underprivileged, ensuring no one sleeps hungry. Volunteers distribute hot meals and essentials to people in need.

50,000+ meals & clothing items distributed
📚

PROJECT BACHPANSHALA

"Nurturing Young Minds, Building Bright Futures"

Bridges the education gap for underprivileged children through school-support, basic digital literacy, life skills and awareness campaigns on children's rights, health and hygiene — in coordination with Anganwadis and government schools.

Quality education & digital literacy for children
🐾

PROJECT JEEV

"Empowering Lives, Spreading Compassion"

Dedicated to animal welfare — rescue, protection and regular feeding of stray and voiceless animals through a committed volunteer network.

50+ stray animals fed every day
🕊️

PROJECT UDAAN

"Soaring Towards a Brighter Future"

Empowers women by collaborating with self-help groups in rural areas — promoting skill development, financial independence and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Women empowered toward financial independence
🌱

PROJECT PRAKRITI

"Plant for a Better Tomorrow"

Advocates sustainability and environmental conservation through massive tree-plantation drives and support for eco-friendly agriculture.

20,000+ saplings planted
💼

PROJECT VIKAS

"Enhancing Employability, Building Skills"

Facilitates employment and skill development through internships in content writing, digital marketing, data operations, finance, research, social work and more.

30,000+ interns trained in 4 years
